Pixel Wayfarer

Android game · Free with optional purchases · Contains ads · Ages 13+

Carry Hearthvale's last ember down the road, relight every beacon and bring the ages forward, from the stone age to a neon future. Walk each road while a growing horde closes in, step off to chop, mine and gather, turn it all into upgrades at the bench, and beat the guardian at every gate. Five worlds, fifteen roads, a signature weapon per age, Google Play Games leaderboards and cloud save.
