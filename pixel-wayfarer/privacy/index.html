Pixel Wayfarer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026. Developer: humardev. Contact: support@humar.dev

This policy explains what data the Pixel Wayfarer Android app ("the game") uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device (plus your Play Games cloud save if you use it). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, usage analytics by Google Analytics for Firebase (only with your consent where required), and purchases are handled by Google Play.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your game progress (upgrades, weapons, gems, purchases, settings) is saved on your device. We do not run servers and never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your save is also stored in Play Games Saved Games (see below). If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this save in your Google backup; that is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of your save in Play Games Saved Games, tied to your Google account, so your progress follows you to a new device, and submits your scores to two leaderboards (Farthest Town and Enemies Defeated). Your Play Games player name and score appear on those leaderboards. Google stores and processes this data; we cannot read it. You can manage or delete it in the Play Games app or your Google account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK collects data from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps), crash logs and other diagnostic data;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in the game under Pause > Privacy (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses advertising data, Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

On Android, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, so we can improve it. It collects:

  • gameplay events: roads started, won or lost, upgrades, screens opened, rewards and gems earned or spent, and ad and purchase steps;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system, language, app version);
  • the Firebase app-instance ID, a random identifier for this installation.

We do not send names or contact data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics only starts after you agree in Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time under Pause > Privacy; until you decide, nothing is sent. Elsewhere, where no consent is required, analytics is on by default. Anyone can turn analytics off at any time with the Analytics switch on the pause screen; the game then stops sending events. The data is processed by Google; see Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and security in Firebase. The public web version of the game does not use analytics.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (a 100 gems pack and Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see them. The game only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play on your device so it can give you the item and restore Remove Ads after a reinstall. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

What we do not do

  • No accounts of our own, and no chat.
  • No tracking tools of our own: analytics is done by Google Analytics for Firebase, as described above.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data ourselves.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Your save stays on your device and we hold no account or name for you. Analytics data is keyed to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or contact details, so we cannot look up one person’s events; uninstalling the game stops new data, and you can email support@humar.dev with any request. For data collected by Google (ads and analytics), use your Google account privacy settings or contact Google. For any question, email support@humar.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
