Pixel Tower Defense - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026. Developer: humardev. Contact: support@humar.dev

This policy explains what data the Pixel Tower Defense Android app ("the game") uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and purchases are handled by Google Play.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, Gems, skins, powers, settings and purchase records) is saved on your device only. We do not run servers and never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this save in your Google backup; that is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK collects data from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps), crash logs and other diagnostic data;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses advertising data, Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (Gem packs, Remove Ads, Starter Pack) are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see them. The game only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play on your device so it can give you the item and restore Remove Ads and Starter Pack after a reinstall. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or chat.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data ourselves.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete; your save stays on your device. For data collected by Google, use your Google account privacy settings or contact Google. For any question, email support@humar.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
